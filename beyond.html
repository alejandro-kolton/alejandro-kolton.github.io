Science meets music

Argentine rock collaboration network

Who are the real hubs of Argentine rock? Starting from the rock.com.ar encyclopedia, I built the collaboration network of more than 6,000 artists (who recorded with whom, who played in which band) and analyzed it with graph theory: hubs, communities, centrality. Charly García is the most connected artist, while León Gieco is the main bridge between scenes.
